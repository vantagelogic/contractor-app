## FAQ

**Does crew need to install an app?**  
No. VantageLogic runs in the mobile browser. Share a link; crew log in and go.

**Can crew edit timesheets after submitting?**  
No — by design. Mistakes are corrected by the admin. This keeps job costing accurate and auditable.

**Does it replace QuickBooks?**  
No. VantageLogic handles field logging and job costing. Export CSV data for your bookkeeper or accounting software.

**Does it work offline?**  
An internet connection is required to sync data. Offline mode is on the roadmap.

**What trades is it built for?**  
Any contractor who tracks hours and costs by project — framing, electrical, plumbing, GC, renovation, and more.

**Is my data safe if my trial ends?**  
Yes. Your data is preserved. Subscribe to restore full access.

**What AI features are included?**  
Voice logging, receipt scanning, site quote generation, estimate suggestions, and an in-app help assistant — all included in your subscription.
