# VantageLogic — Crew Training Guide

Quick reference for field crew using VantageLogic on their phone.

---

## What You Use It For

Log your work so the office knows where time and money go on each project:

- **Hours** worked
- **Materials** purchased
- **Kilometres** driven
- **Requests** when you need something
- **Site quotes** when estimating work on site (if your company uses this)

You do **not** need to install an app. Open the link from your boss in your phone browser, log in, and go.

---

## Getting Started

1. Open the app link your supervisor sent you
2. Log in with the email and password they gave you
3. Go to **Settings** — confirm your name is correct
4. If you see *"Account not linked"* — tell your supervisor to connect your login to your crew record

**Change your password:** Settings → enter current password → new password → save

## Voice Logging (Fastest Way)

**On Home → hold the microphone → speak → release (or slide up to lock for hands-free)**

### Hands-free mode (gloves / dirty hands)
1. **Hold** the mic button and start speaking
2. **Slide your finger up** into the dashed “Slide up to lock” zone — recording continues without holding
3. When finished, **tap the mic again** to stop and process

### Quick log (short notes)
Hold, speak, release without locking.

### Examples
- *"Log 8 hours on Smith House, framing, today"*
- *"Log 4 hours overtime on the Johnson project for electrical"*
- *"Log 45 kilometres for a trip to the Johnson site"*
- *"I need 10 sheets of drywall for the Main Street project"*

### After you speak
1. A summary card appears — check project, hours, work type
2. If work type is unclear, pick the correct one from the dropdown
3. Tap **Review and Submit**
4. Confirm on the form → tap **Submit**

**Tips:**
- Hold the mic before you speak; slide up to lock if you need hands-free
- Allow microphone access if your browser asks
- iPhone: Settings → Safari → Microphone → Allow
- Your voice is **not recorded** — only the text is used

## Log Hours

**Log → Hours** (or voice → Review and Submit)

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| Crew | Your name |
| Project | The job you worked on |
| Work type | What you were doing — Framing, Electrical, etc. |
| Date | Defaults to today |
| Hours worked | e.g. 8 or 8.5 |
| Overtime / premium | Only if your company tracks this — check the box and enter hours |
| Field notes | Optional — what you worked on |

Tap **Submit Timesheet**.

**From your schedule:** On Home, tap a scheduled shift to pre-fill the form.

**After submitting:** You cannot edit it yourself. If you made a mistake, tell your supervisor.

## Log Materials

**Log → Materials**

Choose one of three tabs:

### Store Bought
Manual entry when you paid at a store.
- Project, work type, description, supplier, total cost, date, notes

### Scan Receipt (AI)
1. Select **project** and **work type** first
2. Tap **Scan Receipt**
3. Photograph the receipt in good lighting, flat
4. AI reads the items — review each line
5. Save items you want to log

**Tip:** Works best in good lighting with the receipt flat.

### From Inventory
Pull stock the company already owns.
- Select project, inventory item, quantity
- Creates a **request** — office must approve before stock is deducted

## Log Mileage

**Log → Mileage**

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| Crew | Your name |
| Project | Which job the trip was for |
| Trip date | Date of travel |
| Kilometres driven | Total km (follow your company's one-way vs round-trip policy) |
| Purpose | e.g. *Site visit*, *Supply run* |
| Notes | Optional |

Tap **Submit Mileage**.

## Requests

**Requests → + New Request**

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| Project | Which job this relates to |
| Request type | See list below |
| Description | What you need or what happened |

### Request types
- **Additional Materials** — need supplies
- **Inventory Pull** — take from company stock (select item + quantity)
- **Scope Change** — work changed from original plan
- **Equipment Issue** — broken or missing tool/equipment
- **Safety Concern** — safety issue on site
- **Other**

Tap **Submit Request**. Track status on the Requests screen. Tap any request to see the comment thread with the office.

**Voice shortcut:** Say *"I need…"* on Home → voice logging routes you here with the form pre-filled.

## Site Quotes (Quote Tab)

*Only if your company uses site quoting.*

Build a site quote on site for the office to review before it goes to the customer.

1. **Quote → + New site quote**
2. Select the **project** (office must create it first)
3. Add **scope summary** — what needs to be done
4. Add **site notes** — access, conditions, measurements
5. Optional: **Record voice note** or **attach photos**
6. Tap **Generate estimate with AI** — review the draft rows
7. Adjust hours and materials if needed
8. Tap **Submit to office for review**

**Statuses you'll see:**
- *Draft* — you're still editing
- *Pending review* — waiting on office
- *Returned* — office wants changes (read their note, revise, resubmit)

Use the **discussion thread** on the quote if the office has questions.

## Rules to Remember

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Pick the right project** | Costs go to that project's budget |
| **Pick the right work type** | Shows where labour/materials are spent |
| **Submit same day when possible** | Keeps the dashboard accurate |
| **Can't edit after submit** | Tell your supervisor to fix mistakes |
| **Be specific in descriptions** | *"2x4x8 SPF studs x50"* not just *"lumber"* |
| **Receipt scan: select project first** | Required before scanning |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| App won't load | Check internet; close and reopen browser |
| Can't log in | Try Forgot password; or ask supervisor to reset |
| Project missing from dropdown | Ask supervisor — project may not be active |
| Work type missing | Ask supervisor to add work types in Settings |
| Voice not working | Allow microphone in browser settings |
| Mic denied on iPhone | Settings → Safari → Microphone → Allow |
| "Account not linked" | Supervisor links your login to crew record |
| "Trial ended" | Company owner needs to subscribe — your data is safe |
| Quote tab error | Tell supervisor — backend may need updating |

## AI Help Assistant

Tap the floating **?** help button (bottom-right on desktop; above the safe area on phone).

Ask anything:
- *How do I log hours?*
- *How does voice logging work?*
- *How do I submit a site quote?*
- *I made a mistake on a timesheet*

The assistant knows you're crew and gives field-relevant answers.

## Privacy

- Voice recordings are **not saved**
- Only text transcripts are sent to process your entry
- Your timesheets and logs are visible to your company admin
